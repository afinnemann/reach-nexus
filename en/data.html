Data sources

A curated catalogue of openly accessible environmental and health datasets for São Paulo and the Netherlands — built so that the consortium teams can locate authoritative sources at a glance, compare resolution, period and licence, and trace which environmental datasets can be joined to health-outcome anchors.

What this is

Each card below describes one dataset: who publishes it, what it covers, how to access it, and a short R snippet for fetching a small illustrative slice. Use the filters to narrow by country or domain.

Source registry maintained at github.com/afinnemann/brazil_nl_env_data. Cards are curated; flagged entries are pending consortium consultation.
