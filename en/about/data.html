Data & FAIR

Data-management approach

REACH Nexus follows FAIR principles — Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable — within the constraints set by the ethics framework. Where community data sovereignty applies, accessibility is mediated through the relevant community partner and is not the consortium's unilateral decision.

Repositories

  • Code: project GitHub organisation; mirrored to Zenodo on tagged release with a DOI.
  • Open data: Zenodo and DataverseNL (UvA), with DOIs.
  • Restricted data: held within the host institution; access by request through the data-access committee.

Embargo and access policy

Where journal embargoes apply, the metadata are released openly while the underlying article is under embargo. Accepted manuscripts are deposited in institutional green-OA repositories (UvA Pure, USP repositórios) on acceptance, with the publisher version released when permitted.

Indigenous and community data sovereignty

Data describing indigenous communities (Parque das Tribos) and informal-settlement residents (Jardim Pantanal) follow CARE principles — Collective benefit, Authority to control, Responsibility, Ethics — alongside FAIR. Geocoded community data are not opened publicly without prior agreement with the relevant partner.
