Safeguarding

Safeguarding covers the safety of community participants, the safety of researchers, and the integrity of data the project holds. We take all three seriously and we make our channels public so that complaints can be raised independently of the day-to-day project team.

Researcher conduct

All researchers — staff, students, partners — sign the project Code of Conduct on joining. The code requires informed-consent handling, respect for community norms, and an expectation of safe and harassment-free working conditions in the field. Breaches go directly to the Management Committee chair, with escalation paths to the host institution's integrity office.

Community grievance channel

Residents at any case site can raise a concern about the project — about a specific researcher, the way data are being collected, the way the community appears in public outputs, or anything else — through their Local Working Group or directly to a named ombudsperson within the consortium. The named ombudsperson rotates and is announced on the contact page.

Field safety

Field protocols are signed off per site by the Local Working Group before any data collection begins. They cover travel safety, working in flood-affected periods, working with elders and children, and the specific risks each site presents.

Data-incident response

If you believe project data have been mishandled, lost, or accessed inappropriately, please email info@reach-nexus.org with subject "Data incident". The data-protection officer at the relevant host institution will respond within five working days.
