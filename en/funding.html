Funding

REACH Nexus is jointly funded by the Dutch Research Council (NWO) and the São Paulo Research Foundation (FAPESP) under the Merian Fund Cooperation Brazil – The Netherlands 2024 call "Extreme Heat and Water Events: Mitigating the Adverse Health Effects of Climate Change in Cities."
