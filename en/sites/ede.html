Ede

🇳🇱 Ede, Gelderland Province, Netherlands

Mid-sized Dutch municipality on the agricultural fringe — heat, drought, and air-quality pressures.

How this place is shown here

What this page shows: a city-level marker for the Ede municipality and a plain-language description of the case study. What it does not show: neighbourhood-level vulnerability scores held by the municipality.

Local partner

Municipality of Ede + Universiteit van Amsterdam.

About the site

Ede is a smaller Dutch municipality (~120,000 residents) but among the largest by surface area. Its proximity to agricultural land creates particular challenges for air quality and public health. Climate change brings combined pressures from heat, drought, and heavy rainfall. The municipality's challenges are twofold: green urban development to manage urban heat island effects and water absorption in built-up areas, alongside a rural transition toward sustainable agriculture and energy. Ede already monitors heat-stress vulnerability per neighbourhood.

Vulnerabilities under study

  • Heat stress (neighbourhood-level monitoring active)
  • Drought pressure
  • Agricultural air-quality interface
  • Heavy-rainfall absorption capacity
