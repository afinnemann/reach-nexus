Nelson Mandelapark

🇳🇱 Amsterdam, Bijlmermeer, Amsterdam Zuidoost, Netherlands

Modernist 1960s neighbourhood in low-lying Amsterdam Southeast — flood and heat pressures.

About the site

Nelson Mandelapark (formerly Bijlmerpark) is a central park in the Bijlmermeer, a neighbourhood in southeast Amsterdam with around 86,000 residents. The area was designed and built in the 1960s as a modernist polder development, sitting among the lowest-lying parts of the city and exposed both to flood risk and to localised urban heat island effects. Recent green-space interventions include the redeveloped Nelson Mandelapark (2014) and Brasapark (2022), alongside concerns about gentrification near Amstel III.

Vulnerabilities under study

  • Flood risk in low-lying polder
  • Urban heat island in built-up zones
  • Gentrification pressures
  • Health-equity gradients
