WP2 — Joint Modelling — Designing the Model

Surveys existing climate, mental-health, and physical-health models, then co-creates site-specific causal-loop diagrams and agent-based models. Produces a comparative meta-model and vulnerability dashboards that link climate exposure to health outcomes across the four cases.

Lead
Reinout Wiers
Months
1–47
Phase
Understand → Integrate

Deliverables

  • Review of existing climate-health models
  • Site-specific causal loop diagrams (4 sites)
  • Site-specific agent-based models
  • Comparative meta-model across sites
  • Vulnerability dashboards
