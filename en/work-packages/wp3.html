WP3 — Health Effects of Climate Extremes — Public Policy and Systems Analysis

Brings together secondary data on climate-sensitive disease, post-extreme-event mental-health surveys, and a system-level analysis of primary-health-care capacity in each case site. Surfaces where the health system bends and where it breaks under climate stress.

Lead
Leandro Giatti & Vanessa Harris
Months
3–27
Phase
Understand

Deliverables

  • Secondary-data analysis of climate-sensitive disease across sites
  • Mental-health surveys following extreme weather events
  • Primary-health-care capacity assessment per site
  • Comparative health-systems brief
