WP5 — Innovations in Public-Health Policies and Urban Planning

Develops an integrative urban climate-stress framework, simulates city-level interventions using the WP2 models, and runs pilot living-lab interventions in one Brazilian and one Dutch site. Translates findings into policy advice tailored to each municipality.

Lead
André Nollkaemper, Jannes Willems, Pedro Roberto Jacobi
Months
13–60
Phase
Improve

Deliverables

  • Integrative urban climate-stress framework
  • City-level intervention simulations
  • Living-lab pilot in Brazil
  • Living-lab pilot in the Netherlands
  • Site-specific policy briefs
